Seclude

Permissions down to the single entry.

Restrict an editor to individual entries, categories, assets and users — not whole sections. By hand, or by rules that follow authorship, relations and structure.

Free Craft 5.3+ · PHP 8.2+ · no build step
The whole idea
What Craft can say Jane may edit the News section What Seclude can say Jane may edit these three News entries

Craft's permissions stop at the container. Seclude carries them the rest of the way, to the element — the job Isolate did on Craft 3 and 4, rebuilt for Craft 5.

Safe on a live site

It can only
take away

Seclude narrows what Craft already allows. It never grants anything.

  • Switching it on cannot widen anybody's access
  • Sections no policy names are left exactly as they were
  • A policy it can't read is disabled and reported
Refused
Inside a policy, no grant reaches it
Hidden from the index and refused on the edit page.
Not governed
No policy names this user here
Seclude has no opinion. Craft's own permissions decide.
Permitted
A policy grants it
Only as far as Craft's section permissions already allow.
Never
More than Craft would have allowed
Seclude only ever answers “no”, or stays out of it.
